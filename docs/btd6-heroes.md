# Bloons TD 6: Basic Heroes

Researched 2026-11-28. "Basic player" is read here as the starter and early heroes.

## How reliable this is

- Both main wikis refused automated page reads (bloonswiki.com: 403, fandom: 402), so most detail comes from unofficial guides.
- The bloon.games hero pages were read through a model-written summary, not word for word. They give no game version.
- The Basically Average guide was last updated May 2022 and is probably out of date where it differs.
- Nothing was checked in the game. Verify numbers there before relying on them.

## How heroes work

- One hero per game.
- The hero costs cash to place and levels up to 20.
- Activated abilities unlock at level 3 and level 10.

## Quincy (archer, free starter)

- **Base attack:** sharp arrows. Damage 1, pierce 3, range 50, one shot per 0.95 s.
- **Camo:** detected from level 5.
- **Level 3, Rapid Shot:** 3x attack speed (4x from level 15). Lasts 8 s, cooldown 60 s.
- **Level 10, Storm of Arrows:** arrow barrage anywhere on the map. Cooldown 70 s.
- **Other levels:** 2 arrows per shot at 6, exploding arrow every third shot at 7, 3x damage to MOAB-class at 8 (4x at 14).
- **Advantages:** free, cheap, usable from round 1, handles early camo alone, levels at the fastest rate.
- **Disadvantages:** cannot pop Lead or Frozen, needs help with DDTs, no support or income, falls off late.
- **Disputed:** the 2022 guide says exploding arrows destroy Lead; two newer pages say Quincy cannot pop Lead.

## Gwendolin (fire)

- **Base attack:** fire. Damage 1, pierce 2, range 38, one attack per 0.5 s.
- **Camo:** the main attack cannot target camo. Both abilities hit camo.
- **Level 3, Cocktail of Fire:** burning patch on the track. Lasts up to 12 s, cooldown 30 s.
- **Level 4, Heat It Up (passive):** nearby monkeys briefly gain +1 pierce and can pop Lead and Frozen.
- **Level 10, Firestorm:** burns bloons on screen and gives all monkeys Heat It Up. Cooldown 60 s.
- **Level 16:** can pop Purple.
- **Advantages:** pops Lead from the start, good against dense ceramic rounds, buffs nearby towers.
- **Disadvantages:** cannot pop Purple before level 16, short range, main attack misses camo.
- **Disputed:** pages disagree on how strong she is against MOAB-class bloons.

## Striker Jones (artillery commander)

- **Base attack:** explosive. Damage 2, pierce 10, range 55, one shot per 1.2 s.
- **Camo:** none.
- **Level 3, Concussive Shell:** homing shell that stuns the target and nearby bloons. Cooldown 16 s (11 s from level 15). No effect on Bosses or BADs.
- **Level 10, Artillery Command:** resets the cooldowns of all Bomb Shooters and Mortar Monkeys. Cooldown 80 s.
- **Passives:** Bomb Shooters and Mortars attack faster (levels 4 and 18). Black bloons: 50% chance to damage from level 5, full from level 19.
- **Level 20:** Artillery Command also doubles those towers' damage and pierce for 10 s.
- **Advantages:** pops Lead, strong with Bomb and Mortar builds, stuns stall dangerous bloons.
- **Disadvantages:** no camo detection, Black and Zebra resist early, weak without explosive towers.

## Obyn Greenfoot (forest guardian)

- **Base attack:** homing spirit wolves, magic damage. Damage 2, pierce 4, range 43, one attack per 1.35 s.
- **Camo:** wolves never target camo. One page says Druids in range gain camo detection from level 8.
- **Level 3, Brambles:** spike bush on the track. Pops 50 (100 from level 7, 500 from level 16). Cooldown 30 s.
- **Level 10, Wall of Trees:** swallows bloons and pays cash. Holds 2,500 RBE (the 2022 guide says 3,000), 7,500 at level 20. Cooldown 90 s.
- **Passives:** buffs Druids (from level 2) and Magic Monkeys (from level 11). Slowing totems from level 4.
- **Advantages:** cheap, strong early, pops Lead, homing attack, track abilities help stop leaks.
- **Disadvantages:** cannot pop Purple, wolves miss camo, modest solo damage late, depends on nearby magic towers.

## Which to pick

| Situation | Hero |
|---|---|
| Brand-new player | Quincy |
| Early Lead or ceramic trouble | Gwendolin |
| Bomb and Mortar build | Striker Jones |
| Druid and magic build | Obyn Greenfoot |

This table is the researcher's judgment.
